About EfficientGov AI

EfficientGov AI is a student-built project for the Presidential AI Challenge 2026. We believe that every taxpayer deserves to understand how their education dollars are spent.

By bringing AI-powered efficiency analysis to the local level, we're helping communities hold their schools accountable - because smarter government starts local.
